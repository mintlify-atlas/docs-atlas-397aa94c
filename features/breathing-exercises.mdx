## Overview

Every emotion page includes a dedicated breathing exercise panel. Rather than showing a generic relaxation tip, the app selects a technique that is clinically appropriate for the emotional state — for example, slow resonant breathing for excited energy, or 4-7-8 breathing to ease you into sleep when tired.

---

## Exercise Lookup

Inside `Emotions.jsx`, the correct exercise is selected with a single `.find()` call that matches on the `emotion` field:

```jsx
const selectExercise = exercises.find(exercise => exercise.emotion === emotion)
```

Here `emotion` is the `:name` URL param read from React Router via `useParams()`. Because each emotion maps to exactly one exercise, `selectExercise` is always a single object.

## Technique Descriptions

**Box breathing** (proud)  
A symmetrical four-phase cycle — equal counts for inhale, hold, exhale, and a final hold — widely used to build calm focus and a sense of grounded confidence.

**4-7-8 breathing** (tired)  
A ratio-based technique that prolongs the exhale and extends the breath-hold to activate the parasympathetic nervous system, making it particularly effective as a sleep aid.

**Pursed lip breathing** (upset)  
Exhaling slowly through lightly pursed lips extends the breath and naturally slows the breathing rate, helping to reduce physical tension associated with emotional distress.

**Diaphragmatic breathing** (stressed)  
Also known as belly breathing, this technique consciously engages the diaphragm with long, deep breaths that counteract the shallow chest breathing typical of a stress response.

**Resonant breathing** (excited)  
Breathing at a steady five-seconds-in / five-seconds-out rhythm brings the heart rate into resonance with the respiratory cycle, channelling high energy into a calm, alert state.

## JSX Rendering in `Emotions.jsx`

The exercise section is rendered inside a `<div className='wellness'>` block:

```jsx
{/* rendering the breathing exercises */}
<div className='wellness'>
    <h2>a breathing exercise</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>
        <h3>{selectExercise.name}</h3>
        <p>{selectExercise.description}</p>
        <p>Inhale: {selectExercise.inhale}s, Hold: {selectExercise.hold}s, Exhale: {selectExercise.exhale}s</p>
        <p>Cycles: {selectExercise.cycles}</p>
    </li>
    </ul>
</div>
```

The exercise name is shown as an `<h3>`, followed by the prose description, a single line summarising the timing, and the cycle count — everything drawn directly from the matching `exerciseArray` entry.
