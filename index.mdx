Flatiron Support Troop is a React-based wellness web app that responds to how you feel right now. Pick your emotion — excited, proud, stressed, tired, or upset — and the app instantly surfaces a curated Spotify playlist, a matched breathing exercise, and your local weather conditions so you can take a moment to care for yourself.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your emotion">
    Visit the home page and click the emoji that matches how you're feeling — excited, proud, stressed, tired, or upset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your playlist">
    The app fetches a curated Spotify playlist for your mood, displays the top three tracks with album art and audio previews, and links you to the full playlist on Spotify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a breathing exercise">
    A breathing technique tailored to your mood appears alongside the playlist — follow the inhale/hold/exhale timing to ground yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the weather">
    Your local weather is fetched via your browser's geolocation and shown with a context-aware nudge — like "consider going for a walk" on a sunny day when you're feeling stressed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Flatiron Support Troop requires valid Spotify and OpenWeatherMap API credentials to display playlists and weather data. See the [Configuration guide](/configuration/environment-variables) to get set up.
</Note>
